# Workshop Agenda

Reference format: **3h30**, 3 to 5 teams of 3 to 5 people, one facilitator. A short version (2h) is at the end.

## Before the workshop (D-7 to D-1)

- [ ] Choose the scenario: IT (NeoTitan, HealthNova), sourcing (Mirage Offshore), industrial (Vénissieux) or a Studio-generated scenario that passed the balance check.
- [ ] Check the AI engine in **Settings** (a cloud provider is recommended in class: local models are slow).
- [ ] Create the session with the number of teams and a quarter duration (20 to 25 min recommended).
- [ ] Generate team links (**Workshop invites**) and send them.
- [ ] Ask participants to read the **player manual** and the case file.
- [ ] Prepare the **pre-workshop questionnaire** (see pilot protocol).

## During the workshop

| Time | Duration | Sequence | Facilitator |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | 15 min | Welcome, objectives, rules | Presents learning objectives, the 7 win conditions, the board's role. |
| 0:15 | 15 min | Tutorial and case reading | Each team follows the guided tutorial and reads the case file. |
| 0:30 | 25 min | Quarter 1 | Starts the timer, walks around, answers rule questions (not strategy). |
| 0:55 | 5 min | Resolve Q1 | Resolves, reads one highlight per team aloud. |
| 1:00 | 25 min | Quarter 2 | May inject a crisis if one team dominates. |
| 1:25 | 5 min | Resolve Q2 | |
| 1:30 | 15 min | Break | |
| 1:45 | 25 min | Quarter 3 | Reminds teams to meet the board before deciding. |
| 2:10 | 5 min | Resolve Q3 | |
| 2:15 | 25 min | Quarter 4 | |
| 2:40 | 5 min | Final resolution | Shows rankings and the comparative radar. |
| 2:45 | 40 min | Debrief | Follow the debrief guide. |
| 3:25 | 5 min | Post-workshop questionnaire | |

## Short version (2h)

Tutorial 10 min, four 15-minute quarters with immediate resolution, 30-minute debrief. Recommended with an INTERMEDIATE scenario.

## Facilitator levers during play

- **Timer**: pause and resume from the cockpit.
- **Announcements**: message all teams.
- **Injected crisis**: use once, to test adaptation (it hits immediately and replaces the quarter's dilemma).
- **Commented demo**: show it at the start to illustrate two opposite strategies.

## After the workshop

- Export the debrief (JSON and Markdown) from the cockpit.
- Reset the session to replay it: the run is archived in the debrief.
- Analyse the pre/post questionnaires (pilot protocol).
