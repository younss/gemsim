# Pilot Session Protocol

Goal: before using the game in a course, check that it teaches what it claims to teach and that the target audience can use it.

## Setup

- 1 to 2 pilot sessions, 12 to 20 participants, ideally with mixed profiles (technical and non-technical).
- An observer in addition to the facilitator: they record blockers, rule questions and moments of confusion.
- Same scenario for all teams.

## In GemSim

The questionnaires below are built into the app: in the cockpit's **Pilot** tab, open the *before* questionnaire just before the first quarter, then the *after* questionnaire once the debrief is over. Each participant answers from their team's screen, anonymously (only the team is recorded, a browser answers once). The tab shows the before/after score, the gain, the normalised gain, correct answers per question and per team, satisfaction, comments and the status of the success criteria live; it exports the answers (CSV) and a report (Markdown).

## Pre / post questionnaire (5 minutes)

Same questions before and after. Scored out of 7 (one correct answer = 1 point).

1. A technical (or industrial) debt that is not paid down… a) stays stable b) **grows over time** c) disappears at go-live.
2. Cutting recurring operating costs mostly serves to… a) improve the quarter's margin b) **free investment capacity** c) nothing, it's another budget.
3. Bypassing quality controls to ship faster produces… a) a lasting gain b) **an immediate gain then a growing cost** c) no effect.
4. Before pitching a strategy to a committee, it is better to… a) convince the most senior person b) **understand each member's interests** c) prepare more slides.
5. An executive demanding a concession… a) blocks the negotiation b) **opens an exchange: the concession can be traded** c) should be bypassed.
6. In a crisis, the cheapest answer is… a) always the best b) **to be weighed against the cost of risk and lost trust** c) to be avoided.
7. Modernising a little everywhere rather than finishing a few projects… a) **spreads the gains thin** b) maximises gains c) has no effect.

## Satisfaction questionnaire (after, scale 1 to 5)

- The rules were clear.
- The vocabulary was understandable without technical knowledge.
- The AI executives were credible.
- I learned something applicable to my work.
- I would recommend this workshop.
- Open question: "One lesson you will apply in your work" (measures the fourth criterion).
- Open question: "What bothered you most?"

## Success criteria

- Average improvement of at least **+2 points** on the questionnaire.
- Average of at least **4/5** on clarity of rules and vocabulary.
- No technical blocker preventing a team from playing a quarter.
- At least 70% of participants name a transferable lesson.

## After the pilot

Record observed blockers, adjust the scenario (difficulty, vocabulary) or the rules, then rerun the Studio balance check before any new session.
