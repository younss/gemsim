# Adapting the Game to Other Cases: Plant, Expansion, Offshore

The engine models a generic transformation: a compounding **debt**, a **capacity** slowed by that debt, **resilience**, **compliance**, a **budget**, **executives** and **crises**. Each scenario names these quantities in its own business vocabulary.

## Available domains

| Domain | Debt becomes… | Velocity becomes… | Map elements are… |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| IT | Technical debt | Delivery velocity | Systems |
| Industrial | Asset ageing | Production capacity | Plants, lines, warehouses, suppliers |
| Expansion | Integration debt | Growth pace | Markets, subsidiaries, locations |
| Sourcing / offshore | Dependency debt | Delivery throughput | Teams, delivery centres, vendors |
| Generic | Structural debt | Execution capacity | Components of the organisation |

The **"Vénissieux plant"** scenario ships as an industrial example (plant acquisition), and **"Mirage Offshore"** as a sourcing example.

## Creating a case in the Studio

1. Open the **AI Studio** (facilitator PIN required).
2. Choose the **domain**, the difficulty and write in the language you want.
3. Describe the case in a few sentences: context, stakes, the camps involved, constraints. Example: *"A food group wants to open three plants in Morocco within 18 months; the board wants growth, HR fears losing skills, finance caps leverage."*
4. Run the generation: the scenario text streams as it is written.
5. Read the **balance report**: the case must be **playable** (a victory exists, the shortcut strategy loses).
6. Adjust if needed (scenario JSON), then publish.

## Language and currency

Write your case in your own language: the Studio **translates it automatically** into the other language as soon as it is published (with the configured AI model), and the Studio's **case library** lets you translate it again. Only texts are translated; figures and balance never change. The currency (euro, dollar, pound, Swiss franc, Canadian dollar) follows the case's country.

## The teaching note is written for you

Every case, Studio ones included, has its **teaching note** in Docs & kit (facilitator PIN). The engine plays the case to write it: the winning path quarter by quarter, typical strategies' results, a reading of each crisis dilemma (most lasting answer, cheapest one), tensions between executives, traps and debrief questions. Read it before publishing a case: if the winning path looks absurd, the case needs work.

## Good practice

- 3 or 4 executives with genuinely opposed interests.
- At least 3 debt-reducing initiatives, one 2-quarter initiative, and one tempting EXTREME-risk shortcut.
- One crisis per quarter, increasingly severe, with two defensible answers.
- Always rerun the balance check after a manual edit.
