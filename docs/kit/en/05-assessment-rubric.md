# Assessment Rubric

Use it if the workshop is graded. **The game score should not count for more than 30%** of the grade: a team can lose the game and show excellent reasoning.

| Criterion | Weight | Insufficient (1) | Fair (2) | Good (3) | Excellent (4) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Simulation result** | 30% | Defeat F | Defeat D or partial C | Partial B | Victory A or A+ |
| **Quality of trade-offs** | 20% | No visible logic | Short-term logic | Explained trade-offs | Coherent, quantified 4-quarter sequence |
| **Stakeholder management** | 20% | Generic proposals, board rejected | A few executives convinced | 1-on-1 preparation, relevant pacts | Coalition built, mandate won, concessions controlled |
| **Crisis management** | 10% | Crisis ignored | Cheapest answer by default | Answer fits the context | Answer strengthens the long-term position |
| **Debrief and transfer** | 20% | Story without analysis | Analysis of what happened | Links to course concepts | Concrete transposition to a real situation |

## Evidence

- Markdown debrief export (metrics and quarter history).
- War Room transcripts (1-on-1s and board).
- Optional one-page individual reflection: one lesson, one mistake, one action.
